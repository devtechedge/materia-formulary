# Methodology

## Evidence rubric

Grade A: consistent findings from multiple randomised controlled trials or a systematic review in humans.

Grade B: human evidence exists but is limited, mixed, or small in scale.

Grade C: mostly observational, mechanistic, or traditional use with little direct human trial evidence.

Grade D: no reliable human evidence for the stated use. Included for completeness only.

A grade describes the stated use of a specific product or ingredient, not a verdict on the ingredient in general.

## Source hierarchy

Human randomised trials and systematic reviews rank highest. Human observational studies and small trials come next. Mechanistic and traditional evidence is recorded as such and is never described as clinical proof. Where a label potency could not be confirmed on the page checked, the research note says so.

## Dataset construction

The formulary is generated by `scripts/generate-data.py` from `supplement-research.md`. That research file records 130 entries from NOW Foods and iHerb catalog pages checked on 26 November 2026. Each generated monograph carries the exact listing URL as provenance. Slugs are disambiguated deterministically so repeated product names do not collide.

## Rules

All 30 interaction rules are deterministic functions of ingredient slugs and categories. There is no language model call, no network request and no randomness in an audit. Severity, evidence grade, citation and suggested action are stored with each rule. Findings are sorted by severity and then rule id, so the same stack always produces byte-identical output.

Some rules fire on a single ingredient when the risk is intrinsic to that ingredient, such as preformed vitamin A in pregnancy. Others need two or more matching ingredients. A rule firing is a prompt to review, not a clinical conclusion.

## Review process

Every monograph carries a review date and reviewer initials. The public demo uses the checked snapshot dated 26 November 2026. The database path provisions an append-only review log scoped by `account_ref`.

## Limitations

A product listing is not a clinical trial. Blends are graded at ingredient level where product level evidence is absent. Dose ranges are typical adult ranges from labels and human studies, not personal recommendations. The outcome journal stores local scores only and cannot establish causation.

MATERIA is an educational reference. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace a qualified professional.
