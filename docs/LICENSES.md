## Dataset

Product names, categories and listing URLs are factual references to public catalog entries on iHerb and NOW Foods. The research notes, evidence grades, interaction rules and prose are original to this project. The dataset is bundled as a checked snapshot under MIT.

## Fonts

- Fraunces, SIL Open Font License 1.1, self-hosted woff2 in `public/fonts`.
- IBM Plex Sans, SIL Open Font License 1.1, self-hosted woff2 in `public/fonts`.
- IBM Plex Mono, SIL Open Font License 1.1, self-hosted woff2 in `public/fonts`.

Font files are fetched once by `scripts/fetch-fonts.py` and served locally. No external font request occurs at runtime.
